Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy describes how Please Stand By Studio handles information when using Studio Pipeline Runner, an internal tool that uploads the studio’s own finished short videos to the studio’s own TikTok accounts as drafts. The tool does not serve the public and does not collect data from other TikTok users.

What we collect

For the studio’s own TikTok accounts only, we may store:

  • Basic TikTok profile information, including open_id and display name
  • Access and refresh tokens needed to stay signed in to those accounts

Why we collect it

We use this information only to upload draft videos to the studio’s own TikTok accounts through TikTok’s Content Posting API.

How we store it

Tokens and profile information are stored privately, with access restricted to the studio. They are never shared or sold.

How long we keep it

We keep this information until the relevant TikTok account is disconnected from the tool, and then we delete it.

Sharing

We do not share this information with third parties, and we do not sell it.

How to revoke access

You can revoke the tool’s access in the TikTok app: Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

Children

This tool is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do.

Questions can be sent via the Please Stand By Studio contact listed on its social channels.
